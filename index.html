Projects

MailGuard

An email firewall built at the AIC x CrowdStrike Hackathon at Santa Clara University (Track 3: Attack & Defend the Agent). It screens incoming emails and attachments for prompt-injection and data-exfiltration attacks aimed at AI agents, combining a trained text classifier with an AI document review that quarantines suspicious messages. Built with Python and JavaScript.

Balloon-Tracking Laser Turret

A pan/tilt turret that finds a balloon with a webcam, predicts where it's going, and follows it with an Arduino. A pointer-grade laser switches on only when the turret holds a steady lock and every safety check passes. Uses OpenCV, YOLOv8 and Kalman filters. Software done and tested in simulation; hardware build in progress at the SCU Maker Lab. Built with Python and Arduino C++.

Open Play Rotation Maker

A web app for running club open play. Check players in, enter their ratings, and press Next round: it builds balanced teams for each court, keeps sit-outs even, and keeps partners changing. Also runs round robins and single-elimination brackets. Works for pickleball and tennis, in doubles or singles.

Preflop Poker Trainer

A beginner-friendly trainer for the first decision in No-Limit Hold'em: fold, call or raise before the flop. It explains each answer in plain English, shows 13×13 range charts, deals practice hands against bots that follow the same charts, and brings back hands you missed. Works at tables of 3 to 9 players. Built with React and TypeScript.
